<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Cyberpunk Minimalism**
  
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **High Contrast**: Deep blacks against neon accents to signify "cutting-edge" tech.
  2. **Precision**: Sharp lines, geometric shapes, and grid-based structures to reflect engineering accuracy.
  3. **Immersive Depth**: Use of subtle glows and layered elements to create a sense of technological sophistication.
  4. **Performance-First**: Visuals that look heavy but load instantly, emphasizing the "speed" aspect of the service.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Background**: `oklch(0.14 0.01 285)` (Deep Void Black) - Represents the infinite potential of code.
  - **Primary Accent**: `oklch(0.7 0.2 150)` (Neon Cyber Green) - For growth, success, and "go" signals.
  - **Secondary Accent**: `oklch(0.6 0.2 250)` (Electric Blue) - For trust, cloud, and stability.
  - **Text**: `oklch(0.95 0 0)` (Pure White) for headings, `oklch(0.7 0 0)` (Light Grey) for body - Maximum readability.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Asymmetric Grid**: Breaking the standard 12-column grid with offset elements to create dynamic tension.
  - **Split-Screen Hero**: Text on one side, interactive code/visual abstract on the other.
  - **Sticky Navigation**: A sidebar or floating bottom bar instead of the traditional top nav to feel more "app-like".

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Code Snippet Decors**: Subtle background elements that look like code syntax highlighting.
  - **Glowing Borders**: Cards and buttons that have a faint glow, intensifying on hover.
  - **Monospace Accents**: Using monospace fonts for labels, tags, and numbers to reinforce the "developer" identity.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Micro-Interactions**: Buttons that "glitch" or shift slightly on hover.
  - **Scroll-Triggered Reveals**: Elements fading in and sliding up as the user scrolls, mimicking data loading.
  - **Magnetic Buttons**: CTAs that slightly follow the cursor to encourage clicking.

  **Animation**:
  - **Typewriter Effect**: For headlines to simulate code being written.
  - **Smooth Parallax**: Background elements moving at different speeds to create depth.
  - **Fade & Slide**: Content sections entering with a crisp, mechanical slide-up animation.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **Space Grotesk** or **Syne** - Bold, futuristic, and distinctive.
  - **Body**: **Inter** or **DM Sans** - Clean, neutral, and highly legible.
  - **Accents**: **JetBrains Mono** or **Fira Code** - For technical details and decorative elements.
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Swiss International Style (Digital Evolution)**
  
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Objective Clarity**: Content is king; design serves to present information without distraction.
  2. **Mathematical Grids**: Rigorous adherence to a visible or felt grid system for order and logic.
  3. **Bold Typography**: Large, impactful type as the primary visual element.
  4. **Negative Space**: Generous use of white (or black) space to let content breathe.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Background**: `oklch(0.12 0 0)` (Obsidian) - A solid, serious foundation.
  - **Primary**: `oklch(0.65 0.22 30)` (International Orange) - A bold, energetic color for action and highlight.
  - **Secondary**: `oklch(0.9 0 0)` (White) - Stark contrast for typography.
  - **Neutral**: `oklch(0.4 0 0)` (Mid-Grey) - For structural lines and secondary text.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Modular Grid**: Content organized in clear, rectangular blocks.
  - **Typographic Hierarchy**: Size and weight dictate importance, not color or decoration.
  - **Horizontal Rhythm**: Strong horizontal lines separating sections to guide the eye.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Big Numbers**: Oversized numerals for steps, stats, and lists.
  - **Rule Lines**: Visible grid lines or dividers to structure the page.
  - **Iconography**: Simple, geometric icons that match the font weight.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Direct Feedback**: Instant, snappy hover states (e.g., color inversion).
  - **Focus States**: Highlighting the active section or element clearly.
  - **Functional Transitions**: No "fluff"; transitions are quick and serve to orient the user.

  **Animation**:
  - **Staggered Reveal**: Text lines revealing one by one.
  - **Block Transitions**: Images or sections sliding in as solid blocks.
  - **Hover Inversion**: Black text on white becoming white on orange instantly.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **Helvetica Now Display** or **Unbounded** - Iconic, neutral, authoritative.
  - **Body**: **Roboto** or **Public Sans** - reliable and readable.
  - **Hierarchy**: Massive difference between H1 and body text size (e.g., 6rem vs 1rem).
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Glassmorphism & Aurora**
  
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Translucency**: Using frosted glass effects to create depth and context.
  2. **Fluidity**: Organic shapes and gradients that flow in the background.
  3. **Softness**: Rounded corners, smooth shadows, and gentle transitions.
  4. **Light**: Design that feels like it's emitting or interacting with light.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Background**: `oklch(0.1 0.02 260)` (Deep Indigo) - A rich, dark base.
  - **Aurora Gradients**: Blends of `oklch(0.6 0.15 300)` (Purple), `oklch(0.7 0.15 200)` (Cyan), and `oklch(0.8 0.1 150)` (Mint).
  - **Glass**: White with low opacity and background blur.
  - **Text**: White for high contrast against dark/colored backgrounds.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Floating Cards**: Content contained in "glass" cards floating above the background.
  - **Central Focus**: Key elements centered or grouped to draw attention.
  - **Layered Depth**: Background -> Aurora -> Glass Card -> Content.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Blurred Backdrops**: `backdrop-filter: blur()` on navigation and cards.
  - **Mesh Gradients**: Complex, multi-color gradients as backgrounds.
  - **Soft Glows**: Elements that seem to emit a soft light.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Float**: Elements lifting slightly on hover.
  - **Glow**: Borders or backgrounds lighting up on interaction.
  - **Smooth Scroll**: A very fluid scrolling experience.

  **Animation**:
  - **Floating Animation**: Background blobs slowly moving and morphing.
  - **Glass Shimmer**: A subtle shine effect moving across cards.
  - **Fade In**: Soft, slow fades for content entry.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **Outfit** or **Plus Jakarta Sans** - Geometric but friendly with rounded characteristics.
  - **Body**: **Mulish** or **Nunito** - Rounded, approachable, and legible.
</idea>
