If you've started looking at Zoho, you've probably hit the same question almost everyone does: should you buy Zoho CRM on its own, or go straight to Zoho One and get everything? Zoho makes both sound like the obvious choice, and the answer genuinely depends on your business.

Here's how we explain the difference to clients, and how to work out which one makes sense for you.

## The short answer

**Zoho CRM** is one app. It manages leads, contacts, deals, quotes, follow-ups and your sales pipeline. It comes in several editions, with more automation, customisation and reporting as you go up.

**Zoho One** is the whole Zoho suite under one licence: CRM plus 40-plus other apps covering accounting (Zoho Books), inventory, projects, helpdesk, HR, email marketing, forms, analytics and more. It's licensed for your whole organisation rather than app by app.

So the real question isn't "which is better?" It's "how many Zoho apps will we actually use?"

## When Zoho CRM on its own makes sense

Zoho CRM is usually the right starting point when:

- **Sales is the main problem.** Leads are getting lost, follow-ups depend on memory and you can't see your pipeline. That's a CRM problem, and the CRM solves it.
- **You're happy with your other tools.** If Xero runs your accounts, your inventory system works and your team lives in Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, there's no need to replace them. Zoho CRM integrates with all of them.
- **Only part of the team needs it.** If a handful of salespeople or estimators use the CRM and everyone else doesn't, paying for individual CRM users is simpler.
- **You want to start small.** Getting one system working properly before adding more is almost always the better path. You can move to Zoho One later without starting over, because it's the same CRM.

## When Zoho One makes sense

Zoho One starts to make sense when:

- **You'd use three or more Zoho apps.** CRM, Books and Inventory together is a common combination for product businesses, and that's usually where the suite starts to look sensible.
- **You're replacing several disconnected tools.** If your CRM, invoicing, stock, forms and helpdesk are all separate subscriptions that don't talk to each other, consolidating onto one platform with one login and shared data can remove a lot of double handling.
- **You want everything to share the same records.** In Zoho One, a customer in the CRM is the same customer in Books, Desk and Campaigns. No syncing, no duplicates.
- **Most of your team will use something in it.** Because Zoho One is licensed across the organisation, it's best value when the whole team actually logs in.

## The trap: paying for apps nobody opens

The biggest mistake we see is buying Zoho One for its long list of apps, then only setting up the CRM. The business ends up paying for a suite while using a single app, often without the CRM itself being set up properly.

Before choosing, list the problems you want to solve this year and match each one to a specific app. If you can only name one or two, start with those apps on their own. If the list is long and the tools you'd be replacing are already costing time and money, Zoho One is worth a serious look.

## What about Xero?

A lot of Australian businesses already use Xero and don't want to change, and that's fine. Zoho CRM connects to Xero, so quotes and customers can flow from sales into your accounts without retyping. Zoho One includes Zoho Books, but nothing forces you to use it. Many businesses run Zoho for sales and operations and keep Xero for the books, which their accountant usually prefers anyway.

The decision to move accounting is a separate one, and it's worth talking to your accountant before you make it.

## A simple way to decide

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **Which problems are we solving in the next 12 months?** Write them down.
2. **Which Zoho app solves each one, and which existing tool would it replace?** Be honest about whether you'd actually switch.
3. **How many people will log in to something?** A few users points to individual apps. Most of the team points to Zoho One.

Then compare the options on Zoho's current pricing page, because editions, inclusions and licensing terms change often.

## Getting the setup right matters more

Whichever you choose, the platform matters less than how it's set up. A well-configured Zoho CRM on its own will do more for your business than a half-configured Zoho One.
